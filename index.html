Our Services built around whole-person care

We provide end-to-end provider credentialing and enrollment support to help healthcare providers maintain accurate credentials, complete enrollment processes, and stay compliant with payer requirements.

Provider Credentialing

Provider Credentialing is the process of verifying and approving a healthcare provider—such as a doctor, nurse, therapist, or other clinician—before they can provide services for a healthcare organization or insurance network.

Book a visit

CAQH Profile Management

CAQH Profile Management is the process of creating, updating, verifying, and maintaining a healthcare provider’s CAQH profile for credentialing and payer enrollment..

Diagnostics & Labs

Lab Testing: Blood, urine, and other laboratory tests. Imaging: X-ray, CT scan, MRI, ultrasound, etc. Diagnostic Reports: Store and manage test results and reports. Provider Review: Doctors review diagnostic information for clinical decisions.

Book a visit

Medicaid Enrollment

Provider Registration: Collect provider and practice information. Application Submission: Submit required Medicaid enrollment information. Credential Verification: Verify licenses, NPI, certifications, and other credentials. Document Management: Collect and manage required supporting documents.

Book a visit

Commercial Insurance Enrollment

Provider Information: NPI, specialty, practice location, and contact details. Payer Application: Submit enrollment applications to private insurance companies. Credential Verification: Verify licenses, certifications, education, and other credentials. Document Management: Manage required licenses, malpractice insurance, W-9, CV, etc.

Book a visit

Provider Enrollment Support

Application Assistance: Help providers prepare accurate enrollment applications. Document Support: Collect and organize required credentials and documents. Application Tracking: Monitor application status and deadlines. Issue Resolution: Identify missing information or application errors.
